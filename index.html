Privacy Policy
Effective date: 27 September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the NUMINA mobile application ("NUMINA", "the app", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the app.

NUMINA is designed with privacy in mind. The app does not require users to create an account and does not transmit personal information entered into the app to NUMINA servers.

2. Information We Collect

NUMINA does not collect personal information from users through accounts, registration forms, analytics services, advertising systems, or remote databases.

The app does not collect or request:

  • names;
  • email addresses;
  • phone numbers;
  • precise or approximate location;
  • account credentials;
  • payment information; or
  • advertising identifiers for advertising purposes.

3. Birth Date Information

NUMINA allows users to enter a birth date in order to calculate and display symbolic results and related content within the app.

Birth date information is processed locally on the user's device. It is not transmitted to NUMINA servers or used by NUMINA to identify the user.

4. Favorites and Local App Data

Features such as Favorites may store information locally on the user's device so that selected content can be available during future use of the app.

This information is not transmitted to NUMINA servers.

5. Accounts and Cloud Storage

NUMINA does not require or provide user accounts. The app does not use a NUMINA-operated cloud account or remote database to store users' birth dates or Favorites.

6. Advertising and Analytics

NUMINA does not display third-party advertising and does not use third-party advertising networks.

NUMINA does not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services to track users or their activity.

7. Location

NUMINA does not request, collect, or use the user's precise or approximate geographic location.

8. Payments and Purchases

NUMINA does not currently process payments, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

9. Children's Privacy

NUMINA may be used by children. The app is designed so that users are not required to create an account or provide their name, email address, phone number, location, or other direct contact information in order to use the app.

Birth date information used for the app's symbolic calculations is processed locally on the device and is not transmitted to NUMINA servers.

NUMINA does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe that personal information has been provided to us in a manner inconsistent with this policy, please contact us using the details below.

10. Data Sharing

NUMINA does not sell users' personal information.

Based on the functionality described in this policy, NUMINA does not transmit users' birth dates or locally stored Favorites to third parties.

11. Data Security

NUMINA minimizes privacy risks by processing the information required for its core functionality locally on the user's device rather than transmitting it to NUMINA servers.

Users should also protect access to their devices using the security features provided by their device and operating system.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the functionality of NUMINA or applicable requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
